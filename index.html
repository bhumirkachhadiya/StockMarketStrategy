Strategy & Market Analysis Guide

Learn how to read exact Fibonacci levels, option chain tags, and scanner opportunities.

1. The 6 Core Strategy Price Levels

The strategy calculates exact mathematical Fibonacci rays ($0, 0.236, 1.0, 1.618$) across high-to-low and low-to-high swing cycles. Every level plotted on your chart corresponds to an actionable decision:

🟡 Entry Price The key breakout confirmation trigger point. Long (CE) triggers when price trades above; Short (PE) triggers when price trades below.
🔴 Stop Loss (SL) Strict invalidation boundary. If spot or contract price crosses this level, the setup is considered void.
🟢 Target 1 (T1) Primary conservative target (0.236 extension). Recommended zone for moving SL to cost (breakeven).
🟢 Target 2 (T2) Momentum continuation target (0.618 extension). Ideal for booking 50% partial profits.
🔵 Target 3 (T3) Full 1.0 structural cycle completion. Strong resistance/support zone.
🟣 Target 4 (T4) Golden ratio expansion (1.618). Climax exhaustion zone for trailing runners.
2. How to Read Distances & Status Badges
  • Distance (₹) & Diff (%): Shows how many points and what percentage move remains to reach the level from current LTP.
  • NEAR ENTRY: Current LTP is within 0.10% of the Entry level. Prepare order placement.
  • ENTERED / ACTIVE: Strategy level has been confirmed; position is live.
  • T1 HIT / T2 HIT: Target reached! The chart displays green celebration indicators.
  • SL BREACHED: Risk line touched. Protect capital immediately.
3. Option Chain & Live Strike Charting

Cyan Highlight (ATM): The At-The-Money strike closest to current spot price. ATM strikes provide optimal liquidity and delta (~0.50).

Green Tint (ITM Calls): In-the-money Call options (Strike < Spot) for Bullish trends with lower time decay.

Red Tint (ITM Puts): In-the-money Put options (Strike > Spot) for Bearish breakdowns.

Interactive Charting: Tap any CE or PE contract in the chain to instantly chart the contract's real-time candlestick feed with tailored premium Fibonacci targets!
